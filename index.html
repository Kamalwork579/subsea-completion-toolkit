Prevention Measures
●
MEG continuous injection — 85 L/hr at subsea injection valve. Target concentration: 35 wt% in free water phase. Monitor via flowcomputer and injection meter.
●
Depressurisation protocol — For planned shutdowns >4 hrs: depressurise to <50 bar or maintain MEG injection for duration. Coordinate with production operations.
●
Thermal monitoring — Check DTS (distributed temp sensing) every 30 min during well test. Alert threshold: ΔT ≤ 15°C from HEP.
●
Dead oil displacement — Available as backup for emergency shutdown. 20 m³ stored on vessel. Procedure in EOSP Annex C.
Remediation Procedure
EMERGENCY USE
01 Identify hydrate location via choke ΔP, DTS or flow anomaly. Isolate affected segment at XMT.
02 Depressurise wellbore side of hydrate plug to thermodynamic equilibrium. Rate: ≤50 bar/hr.
03 Batch-inject warm MEG (50°C) at 100 L/hr via annulus line. DO NOT apply heat from one side only.
04 Monitor pressure equilibration. Allow 4–8 hrs for dissociation. No mechanical tools through plug.
05 Resume production at reduced choke (15%) until 30-min stable flow confirmed. Notify Wells Engineer.
