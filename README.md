# Subsea Completion Toolkit

Open-source engineering resources, operational checklists, calculations, and training materials for subsea and offshore well completion operations.

## Overview

This repository aims to provide practical engineering resources for completion engineers, subsea engineers, well intervention specialists, and petroleum engineering students.

The project focuses on operational excellence, safety, and knowledge sharing within the oil & gas industry.

## Contents

### Completion Operations
- Completion planning checklists
- Lower completion installation guidance
- Upper completion guidance
- Well handover procedures

### Well Intervention
- Slickline operations
- Wireline operations
- Coiled tubing references
- Mechanical shifting procedures

### Flow Assurance
- Hydrate management
- Scale prevention
- Well startup considerations

### Operational Lessons Learned
- High-angle and horizontal well challenges
- Packer setting issues
- Well control considerations
- Offshore troubleshooting cases

### Training Resources
- Engineering reference sheets
- Technical procedures
- Competency development material
