# Completion Operation Checklist

Pre-Job

Review approved completion program.

Verify BHA and completion tally.

Confirm packer depth and tubing tally.

Review well barriers.

Conduct pre-job safety meeting.

Running Completion

Monitor drag and torque.

Verify circulation returns.

Confirm packer setting parameters.

Pressure test completion.

Record all operational data.

Post-Job

Finalize completion report.

Update lessons learned.

Verify well handed over safely.
